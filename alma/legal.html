Terms and conditions

1. About Alma

Alma lets institutions issue academic records, lets graduates own them, and lets employers and regulators check them. By creating an account or using Alma you accept these terms.

2. Accounts

Give true details and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Institutions must be approved by the platform owner before they can issue. Regulator accounts need an invitation code.

3. What each role may do

  • Institutions issue and revoke only their own records. They must make sure each record is accurate and that they have the right to share the student's details with Alma.
  • Graduates claim their records, decide who sees which details, and may erase their details at any time.
  • Verifiers may use shared details only to check a qualification, and must not keep or pass them on.
  • Regulators audit the system and may hold or release records that look wrong.

4. Records, the chain and revocation

Each record gets a fingerprint that is written to a blockchain. Entries on a blockchain cannot be changed or removed, so we never put personal details there. A wrong record is revoked, not deleted, and it then fails every check. If a graduate erases their details, the details are removed from storage and the fingerprint stays as proof that a record once existed.

5. Fraud checks

An automated model reviews each issuance. A record it finds unusual is held until a regulator releases it. A hold is a safety step, not an accusation.

6. Acceptable use

Do not issue false records, try to get round the access rules, probe the system for weaknesses, overload it, or use shared details for any other purpose. We may suspend accounts that break these rules.

7. Fees

Plans and prices are shown in the app before you pay. Fees already paid for a period in use are not refunded unless the law says so.

8. Availability and liability

We work to keep Alma running but cannot promise it will never be interrupted. Alma confirms that a record was issued by an institution. It does not judge whether the institution's teaching or marking was sound. To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous 12 months.

9. Ending your account

You may close your account at any time. We may end accounts that break these terms. Fingerprints already on the chain remain.

10. Law and changes

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya. We may update them and will tell you before important changes take effect. Continuing to use Alma after that means you accept the new version.

Privacy notice

What we collect

Your name, email and role. For graduates: the details on each record (name, student number, date of birth, title, result and any certificate file). For institutions: the institution name. We also keep a log of actions, such as who checked a record and when.

Why, and on what basis

To run the service you asked for (contract), to keep records trustworthy and detect fraud (legitimate interest), and to share your details with a verifier only when you choose (your consent). You can withdraw consent by removing the share.

Where it is kept

Personal details are encrypted and stored on several IPFS storage nodes located in the country we operate in. Only a fingerprint and a storage address go on the blockchain. Details are never shared with a verifier unless the owner allows it.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and the GDPR where it applies, you can see your data, take a copy, correct it, object to its use, and have it erased. In Alma you can see and download your data and erase your details yourself. Erasing removes every stored copy of the details. For anything else, write to the contact below.

How long we keep it

Details are kept until the owner erases them or the institution removes the record. The action log is kept for 7 years for audit.

Security

Passwords are hashed. Details are encrypted at rest. Every action is written to a tamper-evident log whose head is anchored on the blockchain. If a breach puts your data at risk we will tell you and the Data Commissioner within 72 hours.

Contact

Data protection contact: privacy@your-domain.example. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.
